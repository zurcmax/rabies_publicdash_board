About these numbers. Counts are animal samples submitted to and tested by participating rabies laboratories. They are not a count of all rabies cases or all animals, and areas that submit more samples will show more positives. Positivity rate = positive ÷ (positive + negative); inconclusive and unfit samples are left out. Positivity is not shown where fewer than 5 samples were tested. The page shows summary counts only; no individual records or owner information are published. Laboratory addresses and contact numbers are shown so the public can reach the laboratories.
